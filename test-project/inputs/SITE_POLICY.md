# Site Service Handoff Policy

## Required handoff content
For every completed service visit, record the unit identifier, service date, completed service actions, start/stop functional-check result, and exterior fuel/oil leak inspection.

## Safety gate
If a safety-critical condition is found or cannot be established, do not state that the unit is ready for service. Record the condition as UNKNOWN when evidence is missing and create a blocker for affected downstream release work.

## Escalation
Stop return-to-service, record the blocker, identify the missing evidence or required repair, route the unit for authorized resolution, and revalidate before release.
