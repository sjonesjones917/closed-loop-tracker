OBJECTIVE

Establish and use a phone-first, closed-loop agent reliability workbook that controls a job through 30 explicit stages from initial job definition through final release and permanent defect preservation.

The system is intended to:

• preserve the exact user objective;
• establish source authority before deriving requirements;
• research and compile atomic requirements;
• resolve requirement defects and conflicts;
• create verification and failure tests before production instructions;
• author and independently preflight production instructions;
• freeze exact candidate versions;
• execute ten independent production runs;
• independently verify every run;
• compare all runs without selecting a preferred result;
• root-cause every material defect at the earliest defective layer;
• convert confirmed failures into permanent regression tests;
• revise only the responsible layer and invalidate dependent downstream evidence;
• repeat complete independent execution iterations until convergence;
• perform an unchanged confirmation iteration;
• freeze an immutable production baseline;
• generate the actual finished product in a fresh production context;
• verify the final product deterministically, by meaning, adversarially, and representationally;
• reconcile process correctness separately from product correctness;
• apply an explicit release gate;
• verify that release bytes exactly match audited bytes;
• preserve a complete evidence chain for every mandatory requirement; and
• preserve defects and regression tests permanently.

BOUNDARIES

The workflow begins at Stage 01 and does not permit bypassing mandatory earlier stages.

Production must not begin before requirements and acceptance tests exist.

A stage marked BLOCKED or NOT READY may not be bypassed.

Independent execution, review, confirmation, meaning-based evaluation, adversarial review, and other explicitly independent activities must use fresh contexts where required.

Independent runs must not see another run’s output, reviewer comments, prior failure explanations, or proposed corrections.

A production generator may not be its sole validator.

Process correctness and product correctness are separate propositions.

Upstream approval is not evidence that the final artifact representation is correct.

A completed run count alone does not establish convergence.

A release-gate decision alone does not authorize delivery; Stage 28 artifact-identity verification remains mandatory.

Only exact audited and hash-matched release artifacts may be delivered.
