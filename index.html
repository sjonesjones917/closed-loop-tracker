How to use this workbook

Work one stage at a time

  • Complete the Human checklist, fill the Stage record, prepare the copy block, and apply the stage gate.
  • Use UNKNOWN when a fact is not established, NONE only when evidence proves none exists, and NOT APPLICABLE only under an objective rule.
  • Do not continue past BLOCKED or NOT READY.
  • Never overwrite a material artifact version. Use JOB_ID__ARTIFACT_TYPE__vNNN__YYYY-MM-DD.ext.

When an event occurs

The workbook's Appendix A–F controls appear inside the relevant stage only when needed. They are not extra stages or a separate checklist.

  • Start fresh run/review records an independent context.
  • Record blocker stops affected work and preserves what is missing.
  • Record material change preserves the change and invalidates affected downstream work.
  • Verify release files compares the audited and delivery bytes at Stage 28.
  • New clean job records a reset without inheriting the previous job's release state.
  • Record received output preserves an actual response or generated artifact.

Outcome words

Requirements: SATISFIED | VIOLATED | UNDETERMINED
Release: ACCEPTED | REJECTED | BLOCKED
